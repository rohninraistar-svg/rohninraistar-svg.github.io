Independent stories, made with intent.

SRV Studios, also known as Shikhar Rai Visions, is an independent film and content production house founded in 2025 by Shikhar Rai and Manshish Oran.

The studio began with its first YouTube series, Navneet – A Journey of Escape.

We believe a strong story doesn’t need a studio system behind it. It needs a clear point of view, a patient eye and the freedom to make bold choices. Every project begins with an idea worth telling and is shaped, frame by frame, into a film or video people remember.

From short films to digital content, the work is guided by one vision: honest visual storytelling that stays with the audience.

Shikhar Rai and Manshish Oran, Founders of SRV Studios
